## 3. Cognism 1-to-1 ABM rows

Applied the filters `Company contains Cognism` and `Ad Type contains 1-to-1 ABM`; the filtered count shown in the intro was **7**. Opened/navigated through the row details (including 1–3 and additional rows).

- The modal initially shows a large light-gray/white placeholder while the asset loads, which can make a card look empty.
- After loading, the opened Cognism creatives were not blank: they rendered real purple/dark ads (PitchBook/Lusha, Varonis, Zoom, “Join your colleagues…”, Dun & Bradstreet, and a PitchBook-style social post).
- Image assets are exposed as Google Drive links in the `Image` field.
- Landing-page links are sparse. The populated links observed were:
  - `https://info.cognism.com/varonis` (seen on two Cognism rows).
- The other opened rows showed an empty `Landing Page Link` field / no `Ad Link` href. This supports the feedback that landing-page links are missing for some items, but the images themselves load once the placeholder finishes.

## 4. Ad Type Tags Explained

The page defines **1-to-1 ABM** as ads created specifically for one company, often using that company’s logo in the image and copy; it notes these can have higher CTRs but are hard to scale and are not for every organization.
